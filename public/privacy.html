Privacy

Culver-Stockton College Theatre Inventory · last updated November 24, 2026

The Theatre Inventory app is used by the Culver-Stockton College Theatre Department to keep track of its costumes, props, scenery and equipment, items it borrows, and items it rents out. It is used only by department staff and students who have been invited by an administrator.

What the app stores

  • Inventory records: item names, descriptions, photos, locations, condition and history.
  • Records of borrowed items and rentals, including the name and contact details of lenders and renters.
  • For each person who signs in: name, email address and role.

This information is kept in the department's own database (hosted by Supabase) and is visible only to signed-in members of the department. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running the department's inventory.

Google Drive backups

An administrator can connect one or more Google accounts so the app saves nightly backup copies of the inventory into Google Drive. When you connect an account, the app asks Google for:

  • Your email address, to show which account a backup goes to.
  • Access to files the app creates (Google's drive.file permission). The app creates a “Theatre Inventory Backups” folder and writes backup files and photo copies into it. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive.

The app uses this access only to create backup files, list the backups it made, and move its own old backups to the trash. Google's access token is stored in the department's database where it cannot be read by app users. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or an administrator can remove the backup location in the app. Backup files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
